GG Backup — Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What the app accesses

GG Backup uses the Google Drive drive.file permission. It can only see and manage files and folders that it created itself in the signed-in user's Google Drive. It cannot read any other file.

What data is stored

The app uploads backup data that is encrypted on the owner's computer before upload. No personal data is collected, analysed, sold or shared with anyone. No analytics, tracking or advertising is used.

Who uses it

The app is for the personal use of its owner only. It is not offered to the public.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files the app created stay in Google Drive until deleted by the account owner.
