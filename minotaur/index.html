Minotaur

Minotaur is the server operations bot of the Europa Elites (EEG) Discord community. It helps the server's administrators manage roles, channels and permissions.

  • It is operated by the Europa Elites team and is installed only in the Europa Elites server.
  • It is not a public service. Members do not need to interact with it.
  • Support: the support channel in the Europa Elites Discord, discord.gg/eegaming.
