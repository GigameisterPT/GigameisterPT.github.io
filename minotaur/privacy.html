Minotaur — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

What Minotaur accesses

Minotaur runs only in the Europa Elites Discord server. To administer it, the app can read server structure and settings (roles, channels, permission settings, member lists with their Discord IDs and roles). With the Message Content permission enabled, it can also see the content of messages in the server; it uses this only where an administrator's task requires it, such as moderation or checking a channel setup.

What is stored

Minotaur keeps change logs of its administrative actions (what was changed, when, and the related role and channel IDs). It does not build profiles of members, does not store message content as part of normal operation, and does not use the data for advertising or analytics.

Where data is processed

Data is processed on systems controlled by the operator. Data is not sold and is not shared with third parties, except Discord itself as the platform.

Use of AI

AI tools are used in support of the development of Minotaur.

Retention and deletion

Change logs are kept for as long as they are useful for server administration. Members can ask for data relating to them to be removed through the support channel of the Europa Elites Discord, discord.gg/eegaming, or by email at gigameisterpt@gmail.com. Removing the app from the server stops all access.

Children

Minotaur is not directed at children and follows Discord's minimum age requirements.

Changes

Updates to this policy are published at this address with a new date.
